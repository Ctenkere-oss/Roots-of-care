Quick answers

How do I book?
Everything goes through the booking page, where you can see live availability and pay the $20 deposit. Messages are for questions — they don’t hold a time slot.
Can I get a quote by message?
For most services, yes — send a clear photo of your hair and say what you’d like. Loc repair and bridal henna are quoted after seeing the work in person, since the price depends on the state of the hair or the size of the design.
Do you take walk-ins?
No. Appointments are one at a time and the schedule is planned around each service, so everything is booked in advance.
How do I cancel or reschedule?
Use the link in your confirmation email, at least 24 hours before your appointment. Inside that window the deposit is not refundable — see the booking policies.
