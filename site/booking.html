After you book

What happens next

  1. Send your deposit

    Straight away, e-transfer $20 to rootsofcare.vv@gmail.com. You have three hours — after that the slot is released. Put your name in the message so it can be matched to your booking.

  2. Confirmation

    Once the deposit is received you get a confirmation with your service, date and time, along with the studio address.

  3. Prepare

    Come with clean, product-free hair unless a wash is included. For henna or jagua, arrive with clean, oil-free skin. Full preparation guide.

  4. Your appointment

    Arrive a few minutes early. The remaining balance is settled at the end, with your $20 deposit already deducted.
