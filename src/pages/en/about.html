About

A small studio in Montréal, built around healthy roots, unhurried hands, and two crafts learned at home.

The founder

Hello, I’m Yamiley

My parents gave me two sets of roots — Haitian and Cuban — and both are in everything I do. The Haitian side shaped the most. In my family, hair was never something you rushed through. It was something people sat down and did for each other, for hours, with care and conversation running alongside it.

That is why I started doing hair so young. It was already part of how we looked after one another, long before it was ever a service someone could book.

Two things have stayed with me ever since: art and wellness. Body art is where the first one goes — freehand henna and jagua, no stencils, a table and an afternoon. Hair is where the second one goes: the scalp first, then the root, then the style. Locs sit exactly where those two meet, which is probably why they became the centre of the work.

Roots of Care grew out of that. Not a salon floor — a calm room, one client at a time, and enough time to do the job properly.

Roots of Care

Why the name

Because everything starts at the root. A loc is only as strong as the few centimetres holding it to your scalp, and no style survives a neglected foundation.

“Roots” is also where we come from — Haiti, Cuba, and the households that taught us how to look after textured hair long before it was a service you could book. “Care” is the part that is easy to skip and impossible to fake.

How I work

Three things I won’t compromise on

01

Honesty first

If a style will damage your hair, I’ll say so and offer an alternative. If your locs need rest instead of tension, that is what you’ll hear — even when it means a shorter appointment.

02

Time to do it right

One client at a time, no overlapping bookings, no rushing the last row because the next person is waiting. That is why the deposit and the 24-hour notice matter.

03

You leave knowing more

Every appointment ends with a plain explanation of what was done and how to maintain it. The goal is hair that is healthy between visits, not only on the day.
