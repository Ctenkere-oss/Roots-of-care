Booking policies

These rules exist so that every client gets the full appointment they booked, and so that a last-minute cancellation doesn’t cost someone else their spot.

Deposit

A mandatory deposit of $20 is required in order to book.

Cancellations

Cancellations must be made 24 hours in advance.

The deposit

A $20 deposit is required for every appointment, with no exceptions. It is taken at the moment you book, through the booking platform.

  • The deposit is not an extra fee. It is deducted from the price of your service on the day.
  • It is non-refundable if you cancel with less than 24 hours’ notice, or do not show up.
  • It is fully transferable to a new date if you reschedule at least 24 hours in advance, once.
  • If we have to cancel or reschedule your appointment for any reason, your deposit is refunded in full or moved to the new date — your choice.

Cancelling and rescheduling

Cancellations must be made 24 hours in advance. Use the link in your confirmation email or message us directly.

  • More than 24 hours’ notice: reschedule free of charge, deposit carried over.
  • Less than 24 hours’ notice: the deposit is forfeited, and a new deposit is required to rebook.
  • Repeated late cancellations may mean future bookings require the full service price up front.

Running late

  • Up to 15 minutes late: your appointment goes ahead as normal where possible.
  • 15 to 30 minutes late: the service may be shortened or simplified to protect the next client’s appointment, at the full price.
  • More than 30 minutes late: the appointment is treated as a no-show and the deposit is forfeited.

If you are running late, please send a message as soon as you know — there is often more flexibility than you’d expect, especially for a last appointment of the day.

No-shows

Not arriving without notice means the deposit is forfeited and the time cannot be given to anyone else. After a second no-show, booking is only possible by paying the full service price in advance.

Refunds

  • Deposits are refunded when Roots of Care cancels the appointment.
  • Deposits are not refunded for late cancellations or no-shows.
  • Completed services are not refundable. If something about your service isn’t right, contact us within 7 days and we will do our best to correct it at no charge.
  • Services paid for but not yet used remain available as a credit for [[CREDIT VALIDITY, e.g. 6 months]].

Before your appointment

A little preparation makes a long appointment far more comfortable.

  • Hair: come with clean, product-free hair unless a wash is included in your service. Take down any previous style in advance.
  • Henna: come with clean, oil-free, moisturiser-free skin, and wear clothing that can be pushed back from the area being worked on.
  • Bring water, a snack for longer appointments, and whatever keeps you comfortable — headphones, a charger, a book.
  • Guests: the studio is a small, quiet space. Please come alone unless you have arranged otherwise, and only bring children who are being served.
  • Tell us about allergies, scalp conditions, recent colour or chemical treatments, pregnancy, or tenderness from a previous appointment.

Health and comfort

Please reschedule if you are unwell — no deposit is lost for a genuine illness reported as early as possible. Any scalp condition that needs medical attention will be flagged, and the appointment may be postponed until it is treated.
