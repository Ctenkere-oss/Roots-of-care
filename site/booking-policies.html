Booking policies

These rules exist so that every client gets the full appointment they booked, and so that a last-minute cancellation doesn’t cost someone else their spot.

Deposit

A non-refundable deposit of $20 is required in order to book. It comes off the price of your service on the day.

How to send it

By e-transfer to rootsofcare.vv@gmail.com. If the deposit is not received within 3 hours of booking, the appointment is cancelled.

Cancellations

Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours in advance.

The deposit

A $20 deposit is required for every appointment, with no exceptions. It is non-refundable — but it is not an extra fee: it is deducted from the price of your service on the day, so you pay the balance at the end of your appointment.

  • Send it by e-transfer to rootsofcare.vv@gmail.com as soon as you have booked.
  • You have 3 hours. If the deposit has not arrived within three hours of booking, the appointment is cancelled automatically and the time is released to someone else.
  • It is not returned in cash if you cancel, whenever you cancel.
  • It moves with you if you reschedule at least 24 hours ahead — once. Reschedule a second time, or give less than 24 hours’ notice, and a new deposit is required.
  • If Roots of Care has to cancel or move your appointment for any reason, your deposit is refunded in full or carried to the new date — your choice.

Cancelling and rescheduling

Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours in advance. Message us as early as you can — the earlier the notice, the better the chance the slot goes to someone on the waiting list.

  • More than 24 hours’ notice: reschedule once at no cost, with your deposit carried over.
  • Less than 24 hours’ notice: the deposit is forfeited, and a new deposit is required to rebook.
  • Repeated late cancellations may mean future bookings require the full service price up front.

Running late

  • Up to 15 minutes late: your appointment goes ahead as normal where possible.
  • 15 to 30 minutes late: the service may be shortened or simplified to protect the next client’s appointment, at the full price.
  • More than 30 minutes late: the appointment is treated as a no-show and the deposit is forfeited.

If you are running late, please send a message as soon as you know — there is often more flexibility than you’d expect, especially for a last appointment of the day.

No-shows

Not arriving without notice means the deposit is forfeited and the time cannot be given to anyone else. After a second no-show, booking is only possible by paying the full service price in advance.

Prices

  • Prices shown on the services page are a starting point. For henna and jagua, the final price depends on the complexity of the design; for hair, on length, density and condition. Anything that changes the price is discussed with you before the work begins.
  • Locs: an additional fee applies to styling when you have more than 100 locs.
  • Completed services are not refundable. If something about your service isn’t right, contact us within 7 days and we will do our best to correct it at no charge.

Before your appointment

A little preparation makes a long appointment far more comfortable.

  • Hair: come with clean, product-free hair unless a wash is included in your service. Take down any previous style in advance.
  • Henna and jagua: come with clean, oil-free, moisturiser-free skin, and wear clothing that can be pushed back from the area being worked on.
  • Bring water, a snack for longer appointments, and whatever keeps you comfortable — headphones, a charger, a book.
  • Guests: the studio is a small, quiet space. Please come alone unless you have arranged otherwise, and only bring children who are being served.
  • Tell us about allergies, scalp conditions, recent colour or chemical treatments, pregnancy, or tenderness from a previous appointment.

Allergies — please read before booking jagua

Do not book a jagua service if you have an allergy or sensitivity to berries, eucalyptus or lavender. Jagua gel is made from the fruit of the Genipa americana tree and the gel contains essential oils. It is entirely natural, but a berry allergy is a genuine reason not to use it — natural henna is the alternative, and it contains none of those ingredients.

A patch test is available on request for both henna and jagua, and is recommended if you have ever reacted to a plant dye or a hair colour. We never use “black henna” or any product containing PPD.

Health and comfort

Please reschedule if you are unwell — no deposit is lost for a genuine illness reported as early as possible. Any scalp condition that needs medical attention will be flagged, and the appointment may be postponed until it is treated.

Thank you for understanding

— Management
