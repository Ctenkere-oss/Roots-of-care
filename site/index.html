The studio

Locs are not a hairstyle. They’re a relationship.

Roots of Care was built around one belief: hair that looks good starts with a scalp and roots that are healthy. Every appointment begins by looking at what your hair actually needs that day — not by forcing it into a plan it isn’t ready for.

Locs are the heart of the work here: starting them, keeping them clean and even, repairing them when something has gone wrong, and styling them for the days that matter. Alongside that, body art — henna and jagua, drawn entirely by hand.

Why Roots of Care

Loc expertise

Locs are the specialty, not a side service. Starting, retwisting, repairing and reviving them is what this studio does every week.

Gentle products

Light, residue-free products chosen for natural hair and sensitive scalps. Nothing that builds up, nothing that suffocates the root.

A personal approach
