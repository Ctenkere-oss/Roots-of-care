About

A small studio in Montréal, built around healthy roots and unhurried hands.

The founder

Hello, I’m [[FOUNDER NAME]]

I started doing hair the way most of us do — on friends, on family, on anyone who trusted me with a Sunday afternoon. What kept me in it was not the styling. It was watching what happens to someone’s hair, and to how they carry themselves, when their scalp is finally looked after properly.

[[TRAINING, CERTIFICATIONS OR HOW YOU LEARNED]] — and after [[NUMBER]] years of practice, locs became the centre of the work. They ask for patience, consistency and honesty, which suits how I like to work.

Henna came from a different place: family, celebration, and the pleasure of drawing something by hand that someone will carry around for two weeks. The two crafts sit well together. Both are slow, both are personal, and both are about taking care of someone.

Roots of Care

Why the name

Because everything starts at the root. A loc is only as strong as the few centimetres holding it to your scalp, and no style survives a neglected foundation.

“Roots” is also where we come from — the traditions, the hands and the households that taught us how to look after textured hair long before it was a service you could book. “Care” is the part that is easy to skip and impossible to fake.

How I work

Three things I won’t compromise on

01

Honesty first

If a style will damage your hair, I’ll say so and offer an alternative. If your locs need rest instead of tension, that is what you’ll hear — even when it means a shorter appointment.

02

Time to do it right

One client at a time, no overlapping bookings, no rushing the last row because the next person is waiting. That is why the deposit and the 24-hour notice matter.

03

You leave knowing more

Every appointment ends with a plain explanation of what was done and how to maintain it. The goal is hair that is healthy between visits, not only on the day.
