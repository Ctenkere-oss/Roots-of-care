After you book

What happens next

  1. Confirmation

    You get an email or text confirming your service, date and time, along with the studio address.

  2. Reminder

    A reminder arrives before your appointment — that is also your last easy moment to reschedule free of charge.

  3. Prepare

    Come with clean, product-free hair unless a wash is included. For henna, arrive with clean, oil-free skin. Full preparation guide.

  4. Your appointment

    Arrive a few minutes early. The remaining balance is settled at the end of the appointment, with your $20 deposit already deducted.
