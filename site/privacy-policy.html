Privacy policy

Plain language, no legal fog: what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do about it.

Last updated: September 14, 2026

1. Who is responsible for your information

Roots of Care — Beauty Services (“Roots of Care”, “we”, “us”) is an independent business based in Montréal, Québec.

In accordance with Québec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (as amended by Law 25), the person in charge of the protection of personal information is:

  • Yamiley, owner
  • Email: rootsofcare.vv@gmail.com
  • Phone: [[PHONE]]

We also follow Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) where it applies.

2. What we collect

We only collect what we need to serve you.

When you send us a message

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if you choose to give it
  • The service you are interested in and anything you write in your message

When you book an appointment

Booking is handled on an external scheduling platform (Setmore), which collects what it needs to confirm your appointment — typically your name, contact details and the service booked.

The $20 deposit is paid separately, by e-transfer to our email address. That transfer runs through your own bank, not through this website and not through the booking platform. What reaches us is what your bank includes with the transfer: usually your name, your email address and any message you add. We never see or store your card or bank account numbers.

During your appointment

We may note practical details relevant to your care — for example a known allergy, a sensitive scalp, or what was done last time — so that your next appointment is safe and consistent. Photographs of your hair or henna are only taken and published with your explicit permission, which you can withdraw at any time.

When you visit this website

The site itself does not run advertising trackers or build profiles of visitors. Our web host records standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and reliability. If you consent to it, we may use a simple audience measurement tool — see section 8 on cookies.

3. Why we collect it

  • To answer your questions and give you a quote
  • To confirm, prepare for and follow up on your appointment
  • To take and, where applicable, refund your deposit
  • To keep your service safe and consistent between visits
  • To meet our accounting and tax obligations
  • To keep the website working, secure and useful

We do not sell your information, rent it, or share it for anyone else’s marketing. We do not use it to make automated decisions about you.

4. Your consent

We collect your information with your consent, given freely and for the specific purposes above — for example by ticking the box on the contact form, or by completing a booking. Where the law allows us to act without consent (for instance to comply with a tax obligation), we only do so for that purpose.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us at rootsofcare.vv@gmail.com. Withdrawing it may mean we can no longer provide a particular service — for example, we cannot hold an appointment without contact details.

5. Who we share it with

We share the minimum necessary with service providers who help us run the business. Each is bound to use it only for the service they provide to us:

  • Booking platform (Setmore) — appointments and reminders.
  • Your own bank, when you send the deposit by e-transfer. We do not choose it and we receive only what your bank passes on.
  • Web host ([[HOSTING PROVIDER]]) — storing and serving this website.
  • Contact form service ([[FORM SERVICE]]) — delivering the messages you send through this site.
  • Audience measurement ([[ANALYTICS TOOL, or "none"]]) — only if you have consented to it.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, for example in response to a valid court order.

6. Information held outside Québec

Some of the providers above store data outside Québec, including in the rest of Canada, the United States and Europe. Information stored in another country may be accessible to the authorities of that country under its own laws.

Before entrusting personal information to a provider outside Québec, we assess whether it will receive adequate protection, as Law 25 requires, and we favour providers that offer contractual privacy and security commitments. You can ask us which providers are involved and where they are located.

7. How long we keep it

  • Messages through the site: up to 24 months after our last exchange.
  • Client and appointment records: for as long as you remain a client, and up to 3 years after your last appointment.
  • Accounting records (including deposits): 6 years after the end of the tax year, as required by Canadian tax law.
  • Photos: until you withdraw your permission.

Once a retention period ends, information is securely deleted or anonymised.

8. Cookies

A cookie is a small file stored by your browser. This site uses:

  • Strictly necessary storage — for example remembering your cookie choice so we stop asking. This is required for the site to work and needs no consent.
  • Audience measurement — only loaded after you press “Accept”. Nothing is measured before that.

Declining is exactly as easy as accepting, and declining does not change how the site works for you. Your choice is remembered for six months, after which we ask again.

Change your cookie choice · You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

If a page here embeds a third-party booking calendar, that provider may set its own cookies once you interact with it. Their policy applies to those cookies.

9. How we protect it

  • The site is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection.
  • Accounts used to run the business are protected with strong, unique passwords and two-factor authentication where available.
  • Access is limited to people who need it to do their job.
  • Paper notes, where any exist, are kept out of public view.
  • If a confidentiality incident presents a risk of serious injury, we will notify the people affected and the Commission d’accès à l’information, and record it as Law 25 requires.

10. Your rights

Under Québec and Canadian law you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything inaccurate, incomplete or ambiguous.
  • Withdraw your consent to a given use.
  • Ask for deletion, where we are not required to keep it.
  • Receive a copy of the computerised information you gave us, in a structured, commonly used technological format (portability).
  • Ask us to stop disseminating information about you — for example a photo — or to de-index a link where the law allows.
  • Complain if you are not satisfied with our answer.

To exercise any of these, write to rootsofcare.vv@gmail.com with enough detail for us to identify you. We reply within 30 days, free of charge in most cases. If we refuse a request, we will tell you why and how to challenge it.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec (cai.gouv.qc.ca) or, where PIPEDA applies, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).

11. Children

Services for anyone under 14 are booked by a parent or guardian, who provides the consent for any personal information collected. We do not knowingly collect information directly from children through this site.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our tools or obligations change. The “last updated” date at the top always reflects the current version, and any significant change will be signalled on the site.

13. Contact
