Book an appointment

Choose your service and a time that works for you. Booking takes about two minutes, and you will get a confirmation straight away.

$20 deposit

A mandatory deposit of $20 is required in order to book. It is not an extra charge — it comes off the price of your service on the day.

24-hour notice

Cancellations must be made 24 hours in advance. Cancelling later than that, or not showing up, means the deposit is not refunded. Full policies.

After you book

What happens next

  1. Confirmation

    You get an email or text confirming your service, date and time, along with the studio address.

  2. Reminder

    A reminder arrives before your appointment — that is also your last easy moment to reschedule free of charge.

  3. Prepare

    Come with clean, product-free hair unless a wash is included. For henna, arrive with clean, oil-free skin. Full preparation guide.

  4. Your appointment

    Arrive a few minutes early. The remaining balance is settled at the end of the appointment, with your $20 deposit already deducted.
